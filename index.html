How We Work

Every job is
planned
before we touch it.

Before a tool ever hits your house, we've mapped out the order of work, lined up the right people at the right time, and nailed down every decision that can't wait until later. That's just how we run every job — no exceptions.

01

Pre-Construction Planning

We walk the job, map out the plan, and put it in writing before Day 1 — what's getting done, in what order, and who's doing it.

Nothing starts without a plan
02

Work in the Right Order

Every step happens in the order it's supposed to — framing before plumbing, inspections before we close anything up. No skipping ahead, no guessing, and nothing gets hidden behind a wall before it's been checked.

Inspections cleared before we move on
03

Client Decisions Locked Early

Fixture picks, finishes, equipment — we walk you through the real trade-offs so you know what you're choosing, not just picking from a catalog. Decided early, before we're on-site and need the answer to keep moving.

Informed decisions, made early
04

Documentation at Every Phase

Everything gets photographed before walls close up. Any change to the plan goes in writing before we act on it. Nothing's left to memory.

Photographed before it's covered up
05

Built-In Buffer. Always.

Every schedule has room built in. Inspection delays, material lead times, surprises behind the walls — we plan for it, so it doesn't throw off your whole project.

We don't cut the buffer to save time
06

Closeout That Closes

A final walkthrough, a finished punch list, and your sign-off. The job isn't done until you say it's done.

You sign off before we call it done

Featured Project

From a sketch on paper
to a finished outdoor living space.

Fully custom design and build — designed by Ridgeworks Construction, executed by Ridgeworks Construction. Full subcontractor coordination across slab, downspout tie-ins, and irrigation, sequenced start to finish under one plan.

The Build
Step 01

Site Assessment

Walked the property with the client to review drainage, grade, and existing conditions — every decision after this gets built around what's actually there, not assumptions.

Step 02

Drainage & Irrigation Tie-In

Downspout runs and irrigation lines routed and tied in before anything gets covered.

Step 03

Excavation & Base

This area held standing water before we touched it. Dug down 6", compacted 5" of stone, topped with 1" of decorative stone — so it drains instead of pooling.

Step 04

Slab Pour

Patio poured to spec — the foundation the entire outdoor space is built on.

Step 05

Finished

Custom patio, planting beds, and outdoor living space — coordinated and closed out.
